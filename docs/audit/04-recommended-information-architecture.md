# Recommended information architecture

**Status: FINAL AUDIT RECOMMENDATION — NOT OWNER APPROVED.** There is no approved implementation structure yet. This is a content/layout restructuring of the working site, not a new design, CMS, visual identity or service catalog.

## Strategy

The current site already makes early trust claims and provides early actions. Its main problem is the distance between those claims and readable, specific proof—and the repeated full service presentations that separate them. New sequence: **clear identity + qualified evidence → relevant service → client proof → responsible person/why → working relationship → remaining questions → optional education → enquiry**.

Preserve the distinctive hero/statement pin-and-reversal and footer wordmark/hover in the default plan. Removing the comparison scene, shortening services and consolidating proof can deliver major reduction without discarding those protected features. A shorter hero timeline is an optional, separately approved variation, not silently assumed here.

## Recommended homepage — exact order

### 01 — Header
- **Purpose:** orientation and a fast path for existing clients or ready-to-contact visitors.
- **Why here:** persistent utility, not a sales section.
- **Content:** current logo, navigation/dropdowns, tel action, contact link. Online Services remains distinct from service explanations.
- **Change:** retain working interactions. Consider a more explicit accessible/visible phone label if space permits; do not add sticky WhatsApp or “free consultation” without channel/offer confirmation.

### 02 — Hero with compact trust evidence
- **Purpose:** answer WHO / WHAT / WHY TRUST / WHAT NEXT without explaining the entire company.
- **Understand:** Ghanchi Investments is based in Navi Mumbai and helps with the existing insurance/investment categories; a real identified person can be contacted.
- **Content:** concise outcome heading, short concrete product line, current action pair, named founder micro-proof. Keep at most two non-conflicting substantiated facts; preferred “Serving since 2009” and “1,200+ clients” only after owner reconfirmation/dated source qualification. Do not add both “since 2009” and “15+ years” as separate impressive facts.
- **Trust strip:** integrated into the lower hero area or immediately adjoining it, not another oversized section. On desktop replace newsletter-card prominence with founder/source-backed proof. On mobile keep evidence visible in first viewport after essentials; don't place it behind a carousel/tooltip.
- **If numerical proof is not approved:** use the real founder identity/photo, office locality, direct phone and a named original testimonial link. **Do not fabricate a statistic. Use another trust mechanism.**
- **CTA:** one dominant service-discovery action, secondary “Get in touch” or an owner-approved equivalent. The header's call action remains. Hero contact has always existed; this is clearer emphasis, not invention of a missing path.
- **Remove from this position:** November 2021 newsletter promotion. No live-review badge, fake partner logos or employee-company endorsements.
- **Copy approval gate:** current “financial advisor”/“Financial Planner” wording needs confirmed regulated capacity. Do not ship proposed replacement labels without owner/compliance review.

### 03 — “Making goal-based financial advice accessible to all.” — retained statement scene
- **Purpose:** preserve the approved visual transition and brand promise.
- **Why here:** remains part of the hero's protected introduction, not repurposed as evidence.
- **Content:** current statement pending the same professional-capacity copy review; no new paragraph or CTA.
- **Motion:** maintain shared `.home-intro`, measured hero height, statement boundary, forward/reverse behavior and reduced-motion readability. Spacer is explicitly budgeted.
- **Tradeoff:** default desktop services still begin around y2,700 / 3.0 viewport heights. The service anchor bypasses the scene. If the owner requires service discovery before ~2 viewport heights by scrolling, separately approve timeline retiming; the audit does not promise both unchanged pin timing and a radically shorter intro.

### 04 — Services: compact grouped selector
- **Purpose:** “Do they provide what I need?”
- **Why here:** do not demand process reading before a visitor identifies relevant help.
- **Format:** three labeled groups with concise rows, existing names, one-sentence benefits and detail links. No hidden horizontal-only discovery, no nine big image cards. All nine services remain reachable without expanding nine panels.
- **Groups (navigation group labels are proposals, not new services):**
  1. **Plan & invest:** Financial Planning; Mutual Funds; Retirement Planning; Child Education Planning.
  2. **Insurance protection:** Life Insurance; Health Insurance; Personal Accidental Policy; General Insurance.
  3. **Business protection:** Employer Employee Insurance.
- **Priority:** individual/family needs precede business-specific cover because that matches present hero/client material, not measured lead volume. Within groups retain current CMS order until owner confirms commercial priorities. SIP is an option within Mutual Funds, not a newly invented service.
- **CTA:** detail links preserve `/services/{slug}`; a single nearby help/contact invitation. Keep direct service preselection links where useful. `/services` is the catalog; `/online-services` is the portal directory, not “view all services”.
- **SEO:** full paragraphs/deliverables remain on existing detail routes. Do not shorten shared CMS descriptions globally.

### 05 — Testimonials: compact named client proof
- **Purpose:** “Have people like me found this useful?”
- **Why here:** proof is more meaningful after the visitor sees their service.
- **Content:** one investment/planning example and one insurance example, approved exact excerpts or short original full quotes; names, initials where no portrait, contextual role/region, archive/source link. Neeta Agrawal and Manju Rajvanshi are candidates, not invented endorsements. Eleven originals remain in the archive.
- **Format:** static side-by-side on desktop; short stacked cards/rows on mobile. No autoplay background, auto-advancing slider or hidden long quote required to understand the point.
- **Qualification:** employer affiliation is an individual's background, never company endorsement. Avoid relying on “without questioning” as ideal client behavior. Preserve source meaning and permissions when excerpting; no silent testimonial rewriting.
- **Stats:** no second giant rating/stat grid. If historical rating retained anywhere, make its basis clear rather than implying current Google verification.

### 06 — “Meet your advisor” + why clients choose Ghanchi + recognition
- **Purpose:** connect advice to an accountable real person and useful ongoing support.
- **Why here:** follows service/proof, before process detail.
- **Content:** real founder image/name; verified role and short biography; two concrete differentiation points drawn from current content (coordinated consideration of needs, implementation/annual review/servicing). Fold in the useful audience/geography statement from FeaturedCase.
- **Recognition:** compact awards/certificates links next to founder. One authentic archive thumbnail can be shown only with approved accurate issuer/date/meaning; otherwise neutral link labels. Do not promote “12+ awards” before substantiation or treat historical certificates as current authorization.
- **CTA:** About/founder biography; optional contextual contact link. No duplicate founder callout elsewhere.
- **Not retained as separate sections:** Before/After scene and FeaturedCase overview. Their useful meanings are merged here, not erased.

### 07 — “Your goals come first. The plan follows.” — compact process
- **Purpose:** clarify what working with Ghanchi involves and continuing support.
- **Why here:** a visitor has now found relevant help and seen evidence/person.
- **Content:** Understand → Assess → Plan → Review with one short explanation each; integrate ongoing assistance and direct Online Services access.
- **Format:** compact numbered steps / accessible existing accordion adapted for size. No hover-only discovery. All step names visible on touch.
- **Video:** current real video remains available on demand, not a full default canvas. Verify spoken/visual content and provide required captions/transcript/description; do not relabel illustrative footage as a filmed client event. Do not add a new video infrastructure.
- **CTA:** the section explains expectations, not another aggressive sales button.

### 08 — FAQ: selected decision questions
- **Purpose:** resolve the few remaining barriers to an informed enquiry.
- **Selection candidates from existing content:** how planning begins; choosing insurance; assistance with claims; what to share in an enquiry; no guaranteed investment returns. Final 4–5 questions should cover actual top enquiries, which staff must confirm.
- **Format:** one simple accessible list; no need for four homepage tabs if only a few answers remain. A brief existing-client portal link can replace repeating its full explanation.
- **Destinations:** retain all eleven answers across relevant service/About/Online Services pages or an approved indexable FAQ location. Do not create empty placeholder routes. Keep risk disclosures independently of accordion state.
- **No invention:** fees/response times/eligibility questions may be valuable but require real answers before adding.

### 09 — “Knowledge for your next chapter.” — compact resources
- **Purpose:** optional depth for visitors not ready to contact.
- **Content:** two original March 2021 article links with dates/archive/adaptation labeling, and a November 2021 newsletter-archive link. Smaller rows, not three more visual cards.
- **Why here:** education supports curiosity without forcing everyone through it before learning service/proof.
- **CTA:** `/blog`, the two current article routes, `/newsletters`. No imported feed or fabricated updated dates. This is one short section, not a secondary homepage.

### 10 — “Let’s plan your next chapter.” — enquiry
- **Purpose:** clear low-pressure next step with working form.
- **Content:** one heading, short relevant reassurance, existing form with its validation/consent, visible telephone alternative. Remove duplicated client/years counters and redundant long testimonial if already used above.
- **Visual:** calm static blue/cream treatment with reliably legible fields instead of decorative glass-shard autoplay. Preserve professional rounded styling.
- **Behavior:** same endpoint, payload, accepted service IDs, honeypot, busy/success/error, service preselection and privacy/terms links. Keep required fields by default. Optionality changes are a separate owner decision and must change both server/client tests together.
- **Honesty:** “Send enquiry” is not a confirmed calendar booking. State response expectations only if staff can meet them. Delivery configuration is a launch gate, not something this layout plan silently provisions.

### 11 — Footer
- **Purpose:** verify real reachability and provide utility.
- **Content:** address, phone/email, office info as appropriate, disclosures, legal/social/navigation, compact newsletter form, preserved large `ghanchi` wordmark and layered text hover.
- **Change:** shorten duplicate mission prose, not the essential legal/contact functionality. Keep archive links discoverable for existing clients.
- **Newsletter:** retain functionality in this plan; owner decides whether an ongoing mailing promise is appropriate given archived issues and delivery setup. Do not hide failed delivery behind fake success.

## Target journey (desired comprehension, not timed user-test findings)

| Stage | Intended understanding / feeling | How supported |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 seconds | “This is a local business helping with insurance/investment needs, and I can contact them.” | Concrete hero/locality, readable actions, actual logo |
| 5–15 seconds | “There is a real person and a checkable basis for credibility.” | Named founder / approved fact source; honest qualification |
| 15–30 seconds | “They cover the thing I need; I can see the relevant detail.” | Grouped services and direct links; optional service-anchor jump |
| 30–60 seconds | “People have described useful help; I know who is accountable.” | Short named client proof + founder/recognition |
| 60–90 seconds | “I understand how we would start and what ongoing help means.” | Compact steps, selected FAQ, clear boundaries |
| Final stage | “I can send an enquiry or call without pressure.” | Preserved contact form, telephone alternative and consent |

The retained desktop cinematic intro can delay a slow scroller; these timing goals must be tested with actual visitors and are not guaranteed by the proposed pixel budget.

## Desktop / mobile distinctions

- Desktop: compact grouped columns can display all nine service labels together; retain rounded panels, local typography, blue/green/cream identity, original founder media. Do not add a dashboard or a rainbow of badges.
- Mobile: group titles and service names readable without swiping; use short vertical rows rather than nine image stacks. One or two short proof items, not a carousel. Keep phone/menu usable and input controls comfortable. No hiding trust solely to meet first-fold height.
- At 810px check both tablet hero timeline and contact form's two-column breakpoint; it is not merely a stretched phone. Test 1200px independently.
- Reduced motion: static decorative media, visible contact intro, no required scroll animation to read proof. Preserve current useful reduced-motion hero behavior; fix confirmed contact regression separately after approval.

## SEO and measurement safeguards

Retain all canonical service/article/About/utility routes and 308 aliases. Homepage reductions should relocate detail rather than remove indexed subject matter. Keep one H1, meaningful H2 groups, direct crawlable service links, descriptive titles and grounded Organization/Breadcrumb/Article data. No FAQ rich-result promise: Google limits eligibility. Preview robots protection stays until launch is explicitly approved. No analytics integration currently exists; any future privacy-aware CTA/service/form measurement requires separate approval and must never log enquiry contents or sensitive financial data.
